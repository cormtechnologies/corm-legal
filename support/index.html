Common questions

  • Manage or cancel a subscription: iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Export your data: Profile → Export Data. You’ll get a machine-readable JSON copy.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete Account. This erases your data across all Corm services.
  • Privacy: see our Privacy Policy.
